About

Geospatial thinking for environmental questions.

I am a geographer working at the intersection of remote sensing, environmental science, and spatial data analysis.

Research with a view from above—and attention to what happens on the ground.

My work uses satellite observations and spatial models to understand patterns that are difficult to see from any single vantage point. I am particularly interested in urban environments, landscape epidemiology, climate variability, and conservation.

Across these topics, I care about making analytical results legible: through clear maps, interactive tools, reproducible workflows, and evidence that can support real decisions.
