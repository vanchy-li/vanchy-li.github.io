04 · READING GUIDE

What this dashboard can—and cannot—say.

OUTCOME

Reported-positive detection

The outcome is the number of reported WNV-positive mosquito samples in each municipality. A zero is not proof that the municipality was sampled and found negative.

TWO-STAGE MODEL

Presence, then count

The expected count multiplies the modeled presence probability by the predicted count conditional on reported-positive presence.

PREDICTORS

Available before prediction

Each June–September prediction uses the preceding month’s environmental conditions, prior-year landscape, municipality area, and county identity. No target-month environmental value is used.

LIMIT

Relative suitability

Unknown surveillance effort prevents interpretation as absolute WNV prevalence, mosquito abundance, or human disease risk.
