Traditional built-up indices often rely on shortwave infrared bands. VBI asks a simpler question:

Can the brightness and color purity of a surface reveal the built environment?

01 · METHOD

RGB becomes a built-up signal.

Sentinel-2 red, green, and blue reflectance are transformed into HSV Value and Saturation. VBI normalizes their contrast into a single index from −1 to +1.

Sentinel-2 RGB→HSV transform→VBI
VBI =Value − SaturationValue + Saturation
Value = max(red, green, blue)Saturation = (Value − min RGB) / Value

04 · READ BEFORE USE

A focused index, not a universal classifier.

01

Validate locally

The published thresholds were calibrated in Córdoba and should not be treated as globally optimal.

02

Watch bright soils

Dry, reflective bare soil can produce a built-up-like signal and remains an important confusion class.

03

Expect dark omissions

Terracotta roofs, asphalt roads, shadows, and vegetation-covered structures may be overlooked.

PUBLICATION
